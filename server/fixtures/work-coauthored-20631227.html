Pretending to be Harry Potter (and Other Heartwarming Activities)

daisyridlay, sharkdododo

Summary:

Horcrux Tom Riddle wakes up in a clearing in the Forbidden Forest in Harry Potter's body. Vanquishing Lord Voldemort is the easy part; being attached to Harry Potter's soul for sixteen years may or may not give one a bit of a hero complex. Pretending to be Harry Potter, however, is much, much harder. Especially when all of his friends are actually kind of nice.

This is a story involving the start of Tom Riddle's (very, very long) road to redemption.

Notes:

Written by Amanda (duplicity) and Hannah (waitingondaisies). Enjoy our spiral to insanity as Tom Riddle learns some sanity.
